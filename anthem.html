A song for human rights activists

Let Freedom Be Our Legacy


Download the song (MP3)

It begins with one voice, completely alone — the way every movement begins. Then the bass guitar joins. Then the piano. Then the drums and the dondo, talking drum of resistance. And finally the entire band — the crowd — until everyone is marching together.

Original lyrics and original music. Made for the fighters, the silenced, and the free.
