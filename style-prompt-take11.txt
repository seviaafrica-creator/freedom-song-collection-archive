A freedom anthem, reggae-Afrobeat hybrid at 100 BPM, minor key. Deep, powerful African woman's alto lead vocal throughout — rich, dark, weighty, commanding, smooth with honeyed grit. The song begins completely a cappella: the lone unaccompanied voice singing the first verse, no instruments at all, intimate and defiant, like a song started in a quiet room that grows into a street. On the first pre-chorus the rhythm section enters: slow heavy half-time reggae, deep fat melodic bass high in the mix, sparse heavy drums joining the voice. First chorus: bright driving uptempo Afrobeat, punchy bass, euphoric stacked female harmonies chanting "we'll rise". Second verse: full groove with the deep bass forward. Second pre-chorus: heavy half-time again. Second chorus shifts to a smooth lovers rock groove, bubbling organ, rhythm guitar skank, relaxed and silky. Bridge: dark, tense, smoldering, restrained drums rising to a roar. Outro: everything strips away to the lone voice and a soft piano, a quiet chanted "we'll rise" fading. One continuous performance, smooth transitions, warm analog vintage production.
