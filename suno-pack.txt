STYLE (paste into Style field):
---------------------------------------------
A freedom anthem, reggae-Afrobeat hybrid at 100 BPM, minor key. Deep, powerful African woman's alto lead vocal throughout — rich, dark, weighty, commanding, smooth with honeyed grit. Opens with grand piano and voice alone, intimate and solemn. On the first pre-chorus the band enters: slow heavy half-time reggae, deep fat melodic bass high in the mix, sparse heavy drums. First chorus: bright driving uptempo Afrobeat, punchy bass, euphoric stacked female harmonies chanting "we'll rise". Second verse and pre-chorus return to the slow heavy half-time deep-bass feel. Second chorus shifts to a smooth lovers rock groove, bubbling organ, rhythm guitar skank, relaxed and silky. Bridge: dark, tense, smoldering, restrained drums rising to a roar. Outro: everything strips back to piano and voice, a quiet chanted "we'll rise" fading over the piano. Warm analog vintage production.
