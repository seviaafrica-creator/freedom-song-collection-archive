A freedom anthem, reggae-Afrobeat hybrid, minor key, built as a staircase of instruments entering one by one. It begins with the lead vocal singing the very first lyric line immediately — a deep, powerful African woman's alto, rich, dark, weighty and commanding — completely a cappella: no instruments, no wordless vocalise, no "ooh" chants, just the song itself. As the first pre-chorus begins, a deep melodic bass guitar enters alone under the voice, round and fat with analog warmth, still no drums. At the second half of the pre-chorus, a grand piano joins voice and bass, playing soulful minor chords. At the first chorus, drums and the dondo talking drum enter together with shakers, locking into a driving one-drop groove as the band lifts to bright uptempo Afrobeat with euphoric stacked female harmonies chanting "we'll rise". From the second verse onward it is the entire full band: punchy bass, crisp drums, dondo talking drum, bubbling organ, rhythm guitar, shakers, lush pads. Second pre-chorus: heavy half-time. Second chorus: smooth lovers rock groove, silky and relaxed. Bridge: dark, tense, smoldering, rising to a roar. Outro: strips back to voice with soft piano, a quiet chanted "we'll rise" fading. One continuous performance, smooth seamless layering, warm analog vintage production.
